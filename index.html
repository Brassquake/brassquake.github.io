Welcome to Brassquake

Experience the power of brass from Dr. G.W. Williams Secondary School!


Brassquake is a brass quintet made up of five dedicated high school musicians. We perform a range of repertoire ranging from classical to contemporary arrangements, delivering engaging performances tailored for audiences of all ages.

About Brassquake

Founded in 2025, Brassquake represents the finest brass musicians from Dr. G.W. Williams Secondary School. What began under the guidance of our music director, Scott Harrison, quickly evolved into a high-level ensemble. Beyond our musicality, we have built a close friendship that is reflected in the energy of our live shows.


The Brassquake Team at the GW Williams Music Banquet - (June 12th, 2025)

We have had the privilege of performing at various venues both within our school community and around the Aurora area. Our diverse repertoire aims to showcase the versatility and brilliance of brass instrumentation while inspiring every audience we play for. With a profound passion for music, we look forward to the years ahead, continuing to create exceptional music for ourselves and our audiences.

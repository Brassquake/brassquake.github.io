About Brassquake

Founded in 2025, Brassquake is filled with the finest brass musicians from Dr. G.W. Williams Secondary School. Our quintet brings together five dedicated students who share a passion for music and performance excellence.


The Brassquake Team at the GW Williams Music Banquet - (June 12th, 2025)

Brassquake first formed in early 2025 with the help of our music director, Scott Harrison. At the time, we did not really know what we were getting into, but over time, Brassquake grew close together, and is now not only a high-level ensemble, but also a close group of friends! Over our time together, Brassquake has performed at numerous events both within and outside of our school for numerous audiences. We play a wide variety of repertoire ranging from classical works to modern arrangements, film scores, and popular music. We want to showcase the greatness of brass instruments while inspiring our audience. With the ensemble having a great passion for music, we look forward to the rest of our years together, and will continue to make great music for ourselves and for our audiences!
